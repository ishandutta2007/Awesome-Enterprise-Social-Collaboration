# Awesome-Enterprise-Social-Collaboration

# Top Enterprise Social Collaboration Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Team Communication, Internal Social Networks & Digital Workplaces*  
**Last updated: March 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Enterprise Social Collaboration**. These tools enable organizations to connect employees through messaging, social feeds, community spaces, and collaborative workspaces—fostering engagement, knowledge sharing, and cross-team communication.

**Examples** include Salesforce Chatter, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Workplace from Meta, Yammer (Viva Engage), Jostle, Igloo Software, Simpplr, Blink, and Workvivo (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, data sovereignty, and open collaboration infrastructure—ideal for enterprises, government agencies, and organizations seeking transparent, vendor-independent social collaboration.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Salesforce Chatter](https://www.salesforce.com/)**  
  Enterprise social network native to Salesforce. Features profiles, feeds, groups, and file sharing integrated with CRM records. Enables collaboration on accounts, opportunities, and cases directly within Salesforce.

- **[Slack](https://slack.com/)**  
  Channel-based messaging platform with huddles, canvas, and 2,600+ app integrations. Features Slack Connect for cross-organization collaboration and AI-powered search via Slack AI.

- **[Microsoft Teams](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-teams/group-chat-software)**  
  Unified communication and collaboration platform integrated with Microsoft 365. Features channels, chats, meetings, file sharing, and Viva Engage (formerly Yammer) for enterprise social networking.

- **[Workplace from Meta](https://www.workplace.com/)**  
  Enterprise social network (discontinued September 2025, read-only through May 2026). Features groups, feeds, live video, and chat for internal communications. Meta named Workvivo as its only preferred migration partner. Data deletion occurred June 1, 2026 .

- **[Yammer (Viva Engage)](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-viva/engage)**  
  Enterprise social network within Microsoft 365. Features communities, storylines, and announcements for company-wide communication. Now branded as Viva Engage.

- **[Jostle](https://www.jostle.me/)**  
  Employee experience platform with social intranet, news feeds, and people directory. Focuses on employee engagement and internal communications.

- **[Igloo Software](https://www.igloosoftware.com/)**  
  Digital workplace platform with intranet, collaboration, and knowledge management. Features social feeds, blogs, wikis, and document collaboration.

- **[Simpplr](https://www.simpplr.com/)**  
  AI-powered intranet and internal communications platform. Features modern intranet, targeted communications, employee search, and knowledge discovery.

- **[Blink](https://www.joinblink.com/)**  
  Employee engagement and communications platform. Features news feed, chat, and people directory for frontline and deskless workers.

- **[Workvivo (Zoom)](https://www.workvivo.com/)**  
  Communication-led engagement platform acquired by Zoom. Meta's only preferred Workplace migration partner. Features social intranet, employee communications, mobile app, video, live streams, and recognition. Pricing starts at $20,000/year for 250-2,000 employees .

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[HumHub](https://github.com/humhub/humhub)**  
  Open-source enterprise social network (AGPL-3.0) built in PHP on Yii2. "Workplace and Yammer's pattern on your own server, with GDPR compliance by construction." Features Spaces for departments/projects with granular permissions, rich content (posts, wikis, media, events, tasks), activity streams, and ~80 install-and-activate modules including Calendar, Wiki, Polls, Tasks, Gallery, direct-message Mail, OnlyOffice document editing, LDAP/SAML/JWT SSO, and RESTful API. Serves corporate intranets, city governments, universities, political parties, and nonprofits. Plain LAMP stack (PHP 8.1+, MySQL/MariaDB) makes it easy to operate long-term .

- **[Mattermost](https://github.com/mattermost/mattermost)**  
  Open-core, self-hosted collaboration platform (MIT for core) designed for defense, intelligence, and critical infrastructure enterprises. Features channels, direct messages, file sharing, playbooks, and workflow automation. Offers encryption at rest and in transit, granular access controls, audit logs, and compliance with GDPR, HIPAA, and ISO 27001. Positioned as ideal Workplace replacement with migration tools and documentation .

- **[eXo Platform](https://github.com/exoplatform/platform)**  
  Open-source digital workplace (AGPL-3.0) connecting, aligning, and engaging teams around a single interface. Features social collaboration, company communication, knowledge management, and peer-to-peer engagement. Available as SaaS or self-hosted .

- **[Simoona](https://github.com/VismaLietuva/simoona)**  
  Smart and intuitive open-source social intranet (C#) by Visma. Features Wall for social sharing, Employee directory with profiles, Kudos peer recognition system, and social networking capabilities. Designed for effective communication, empowerment, acknowledgement, and social networking of company employees .

- **[Buzz (Block)](https://github.com/block/buzz)**  
  Open-source workspace (Apache-2.0) where humans and AI agents share the same rooms, built on the decentralized Nostr protocol. Each participant (human or agent) holds a cryptographic keypair providing portable, verifiable identity. Features channels, threads, DMs, voice, media sharing, Git repositories, and automated workflows. Agents can work with repositories, submit code changes, run reviews, and coordinate other agents. Model-agnostic—supports Codex, Claude Code, Goose. Desktop apps for Windows, macOS, Linux. Still early—mobile apps and push notifications under development .

- **[Cobase](https://github.com/CoBase/cobase)**  
  Open-source corporate social media portal (PHP/Symfony2) positioned as alternative to Yammer and Moovia. Users create groups and post messages; each user subscribes to groups of interest. Features user management (login, registration, confirmation, password retrieval), ACL for entities, Gravatar integration, group creation, post like/unlike, commenting, RSS feeds, Twitter hashtag integration, and responsive layout .

- **[Discourse](https://github.com/discourse/discourse)**  
  Open-source discussion platform (GPL-2.0) for community building. Features threaded discussions, categories, tags, real-time chat, and extensive plugin ecosystem. Suitable for enterprise communities, knowledge sharing, and announcements .

- **[Movim](https://github.com/movim/movim)**  
  Open-source social and chat platform (AGPL-3.0) based on XMPP. Enables self-hosting, cross-network chat, video calls, and group collaboration. Users control and migrate their data, communicate across various networks, and access services from any node while preserving privacy .

- **[Apache Answer](https://github.com/apache/answer)**  
  Open-source Q&A platform (Apache-2.0) for teams to build knowledge bases, forums, and help centers. 15.7k+ GitHub stars. Modern alternative for enterprise internal knowledge sharing .

- **[NodeBB](https://github.com/NodeBB/NodeBB)**  
  Open-source forum platform (GPL-3.0) with real-time discussions, built-in chat, SSO options, plugins, and flexible theming. Backed by Redis, MongoDB, or PostgreSQL. 15.2k+ GitHub stars .

- **[Zulip](https://github.com/zulip/zulip)**  
  Open-source team chat (Apache-2.0) with topic-based threading. Features organized conversations, integrations, and self-hosting. Strong for asynchronous collaboration.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **[Rocket.Chat](https://github.com/RocketChat/Rocket.Chat)** — Open-source team communication (MIT) with omnichannel engagement and bot framework.
- **[Element](https://github.com/vector-im/element-web)** — Matrix-based collaboration client (Apache-2.0) for decentralized communication.
- **[Nextcloud](https://github.com/nextcloud/server)** — Open-source collaboration platform (AGPL-3.0) with Talk, Deck, and social features.
- **[Loomio](https://github.com/loomio/loomio)** — Collaborative decision-making tool (AGPL-3.0) for transparent group decisions .
- **[Elgg](https://github.com/Elgg/Elgg)** — Modular social networking platform (GPL-2.0) for building collaborative communities .
- Community **n8n** workflows for collaboration automation and cross-platform notifications.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **HumHub** or **Mattermost** as the core collaboration platform with **Buzz** for AI-agent-inclusive workspaces, **eXo Platform** for digital workplace integration, **Discourse** or **Apache Answer** for knowledge sharing, and **PostgreSQL** for persistence—creating a fully self-hosted, enterprise-grade social collaboration platform.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Enterprise social collaboration platforms handling employee communications must comply with data protection regulations (GDPR, CCPA, etc.) and labor regulations where applicable.
- Self-hosted open-source solutions require proper security hardening, backup strategies, and ongoing maintenance. The Workplace from Meta shutdown (data deleted June 1, 2026) illustrates vendor lock-in risks—open-source platforms cannot be discontinued unilaterally .
